# Neo Moonlight (Vision Pro XrOS Fork)

[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-GPL--3.0-blue.svg)](https://github.com/NeoVectorX/NeoMoonlight/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) [![TestFlight](https://img.shields.io/badge/TestFlight-Join%20Beta-orange.svg)](https://testflight.apple.com/join/maak7yCK)

**Neo Moonlight** is a community‑fork of [RikuKunMS2's moonlight-ios-vision](https://github.com/RikuKunMS2/moonlight-ios-vision) and the original [Moonlight iOS](https://github.com/moonlight-stream/moonlight-ios) project, enhanced specifically for the Apple Vision Pro.

## 🌙 What is Neo Moonlight?
- Neo Moonlight turns your Vision Pro into the ultimate PC monitor — stream games from your PC with the ability to scale up to a giant theater screen when you want full immersion. Curve the display for a more cinematic feel or use adjustable lighting for that perfect setting. Tilt the screen to get an angle that feels right — even lying down on the couch. Enjoy games like Streets of Rage or Lego Star Wars? Neo even has the ability to play couch co-op games with another Vision Pro player. It’s PC streaming, but tuned for the Vision Pro.

## 🚀 Neo Moonlight v12.2 — Lunar Edition
**Release Date:** June 2026

### 🖥️ Display & Screen Adjust
* **Screen Adjust** — Tilt, rotation, and curvature controls for Curved Display mode.
* **Screen Presets** — Save up to 3 favorite screen positions (scale, curvature, tilt). Long press to cycle quickly.
* **Follow Mode** — Screen stays with you while moving around. Use Screen Adjust to reposition; hold Digital Crown to reset.
* **Pass-Through Mode** — Temporarily dim display and audio in Flat and Curved modes so you can see and hear your surroundings.

### 🕹️ Controls & Input
* **Accessory Mode** — Renamed from Controller Mode; updated for gamepad and mouse control.
* **PlayStation as Xbox** — Optional setting to report a PlayStation controller as Xbox for broader XInput game compatibility (gamepad connected to Vision Pro Bluetooth).
* **Scroll or Marquee** — Choose pinch-and-drag behavior in apps, with an optional stream-bar quick toggle.
* **Magic Trackpad (Flat Display)** — Pointer move and two-finger scroll support.
* **Enhanced Desktop Actions** — Function keys, more Windows shortcuts, and a macOS-specific page (App Switch, Spotlight, Mission Control, Spaces, Force Quit).

### 🛠️ Stream Quality & Reliability
* **Bitrate Assistant** — Optional in-stream button to run a live scan during gameplay and dial in the best bitrate for your connection.
* **Mic Streamer compatibility** mode in settings.
* **Bug fix:** Screen position and scale on first launch after a fresh install in Curved Display mode.

## 🚀 Neo Moonlight v12.1 — Plato Edition
**Release Date:** May 2026

* **HDR overhaul** — Reworked PQ decoding, gamut mapping, and tone shaping; in-stream Enhanced HDR panel and optional Reference HDR with three named presets.
* **19 new app themes** via the main menu moon toggle.
* **Reactive lighting** and bloom adjustments with glow reach tiers.
* **600R curvature** option, custom bitrate in settings, stream mute toggle, and Light Mode for the stream control bar.
* **360° environment and lighting** choice persists between sessions.
* **Updated Streaming Guide** with tabbed Get Started walkthrough.
